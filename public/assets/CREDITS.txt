Sound
-----
normalDrop.mp3, magicDrop.mp3   — what an ordinary and a magic drop sound like
rareDrop.wav, rareDropBackground.wav
                                — the two recordings a rare drop is made of
rareDrop.mp3                    — the two above, mixed down to one file:

    ffmpeg -i rareDrop.wav -i rareDropBackground.wav \
      -filter_complex "[0:a][1:a]amix=inputs=2:duration=longest\
:dropout_transition=0:normalize=0,alimiter=limit=0.85:level=false,volume=0.95" \
      -c:a libmp3lame -q:a 3 rareDrop.mp3

  Mixed rather than played together so there is nothing to keep in step at
  playback time. Re-run the line above after replacing either source.
